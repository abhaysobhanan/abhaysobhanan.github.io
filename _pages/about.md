I am an Assistant Professor in the Decision Sciences area and a Young Faculty Research Chair at the [Indian Institute of Management Bangalore](https://www.iimb.ac.in). I develop operations research and machine learning methods for complex optimization problems in transportation, warehousing and supply chain management.

I lead the [CORBIT Lab](/team/). I completed my PhD in Industrial Engineering at the University of South Florida in July 2025, advised by [Hadi Gard](http://www.eng.usf.edu/~hcharkhgard/) and [Changhyun Kwon](https://www.chkwon.net). My dissertation developed methods for large-scale vehicle routing problems and took on practical concerns such as allocating workload fairly. I also hold a BS–MS dual degree in Mathematics from the National Institute of Technology Agartala (2019).
