## Operations research

- [INFORMS](https://www.informs.org) is the largest international association for researchers and professionals in OR and related fields. It runs conferences and publishes journals, news, job postings and other resources.
- [Subject to](https://www.youtube.com/@Subjectto_) is a series of conversations with prominent researchers in operations research and management, hosted by [Anand Subramanian](https://sites.google.com/view/anand-subramanian/).
- [Julia Programming for Operations Research](https://www.chkwon.net/julia/) by [Changhyun Kwon](https://www.chkwon.net) is an introduction to programming, with simple examples that solve well-known optimization problems.
- Solvers for your optimization problems:
  - commercial: CPLEX, Gurobi Optimizer
  - open source: SCIP (MIP, MINLP), GLPK, Google OR-Tools, VROOM (vehicle routing)
- [VeRoViz](https://veroviz.org) is an open-source package for visualizing vehicle routing problems and generating their data.

## General

- [The importance of stupidity in scientific research](https://doi.org/10.1242/jcs.033340) by Martin A. Schwartz is worth reading for anyone pondering the unanswered questions in their field.
- [Why You Should De-Optimize Your Life](https://youtu.be/cqdm9z3oF0c): Maria Cano on the role of occasional de-optimization in long-term productivity.

## For PhD students

- LaTeX is the standard writing tool in mathematics, engineering and related fields. Beginners can start with [this tutorial](https://stom.chkwon.net/latex/).
- Git: if you write code often, or plan to, learn this version control system. [This tutorial](https://www.w3schools.com/git/default.asp) covers the basics.
- The [SEDAC Population Estimation Service](https://beta.sedac.ciesin.columbia.edu/data/collection/gpw-v4/population-estimation-service) estimates population statistics within an area you draw.
- [A Happy PhD](https://ahappyphd.org) is a blog about doctoral productivity, supervision and wellbeing.
- [Scriptoria](https://www.scriptoria.org/en/) collects articles that guide PhD students through writing scientific papers.
- [Fontjoy](https://fontjoy.com) generates font pairings for presentation slides.
- [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) is a large library of high-resolution photos free for personal and commercial use.
- [Napkin AI](https://napkin.ai) offers AI-powered help with refining presentation slides.
